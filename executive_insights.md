# Executive Insights — AI Customer Support & Voice of Customer

## KPI Snapshot
- Support tickets: 12,000
- Negative sentiment: 45.8%
- Average resolution time: 34.1 hours
- Average CSAT: 3.21/5
- Escalation rate: 3.9%

## Key Findings
- Top customer pain point by negative sentiment: **App Bug**
- Channel requiring attention: **Email**
- Product with highest negative-sentiment rate: **Mobile App**

## Recommended Actions
1. Prioritize root-cause fixes for high-volume negative issue types.
2. Use AI-assisted summaries and intent classification for repetitive tickets.
3. Route Critical and High-priority tickets to faster queues.
4. Monitor sentiment and CSAT by product and channel.
5. Add real-time topic clustering and agent-assist recommendations when real ticket data is available.

## Responsible AI
Generative AI can assist with summarization, intent classification, theme extraction and response drafting. Human review remains required for customer-facing decisions.
